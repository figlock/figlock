Support

Help for the FigLock iPhone app, the Mac app and the command line. FigLock is run by a small team, so there is no ticket queue. Write to us and a person answers.

How to reach us

  • Email support@figlock.com for questions and bugs.
  • Open an issue or a discussion on GitHub if you want the answer to help others.
  • To report a security problem, write to security@figlock.com.
  • Never send your master password, Secret Key, Recovery Key or real vault contents. We will never ask for them.

I forgot my master password

FigLock can't reset it, because we never have it. If you saved your Recovery Key, choose "I forgot my password or Secret Key" on the lock screen, enter the Recovery Key, and set a new master password. You can turn the Recovery Key on in Settings, so do it while you can still unlock the vault. Without a Recovery Key, a forgotten master password can't be recovered.

I lost my Secret Key or my Emergency Kit

On a device where FigLock is already unlocked, open Settings, choose Emergency Kit and make a new copy. If every device is locked out and you have neither the Secret Key nor the Recovery Key, the vault can't be opened by anyone, including us. You can remove it from your phone and start a new one.

Adding my iPhone to an existing vault

On the welcome screen choose "Join my vault by pairing". Scan the code, or type the code, shown in FigLock on your other device. Then approve the iPhone on that device. Pairing needs the vault's storage, such as Google Drive or a synced folder, to be reachable from both devices.

Turning on AutoFill

On your iPhone, open Settings, then General, then AutoFill & Passwords, and turn on FigLock. AutoFill needs Face ID and reads your vault on the phone only. It never writes to it.

My vault is not syncing

Open Settings in FigLock and look under "Where your vault is kept" to see each place the vault syncs to and whether it is working. Check that you are online and, for Google Drive, that you are still signed in. If you chose a folder in the Files app, make sure that folder is downloaded and not paused by its provider. A change made on your phone shows as "not synced" until it has been sent.

Removing my vault

In Settings, "Remove this vault from this iPhone" deletes the copy on the phone only. To delete the copy in Google Drive, choose "Delete from Google Drive" in Settings, or remove FigLock's hidden app folder from your Google account settings. Deleting your vault files from your storage and all your devices removes your data for good.

Face ID or the keychain does not work

Face ID is an extra way to open the vault on one phone. If it stops working, for example after you change your Face ID in iOS, unlock with your master password. FigLock never receives your face data. iOS checks it and tells the app only whether it matched.
