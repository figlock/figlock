Privacy

Draft for the prototype. Last updated 1 November 2026.

FigLock is built so that we don't hold your data. This page says what that means in practice, for this website and for the product as it is planned.

Your vault

Your vault is encrypted on your device before it goes anywhere. It is stored as ciphertext in a place you choose, such as Google Drive, iCloud Drive or a folder you sync yourself. We don't run a server or a database that holds your vault, and we can't read it.

Titles, URLs and usernames are encrypted too. Your storage provider can see file counts, sizes and times, and nothing else.

Your keys

Your master password and your Secret Key never leave your devices. We can't reset them and we can't recover them for you. That is why FigLock asks you to save an Emergency Kit.

AI agents

An agent can use an item only after you approve it on a prompt that FigLock draws itself. Each approval names the agent, the item, the allowed hosts and the time limit. Every use goes into an encrypted audit log that only you can read.

This website

  • It is a static site. It has no accounts, no forms and no cookies.
  • It loads no analytics, advertising or third-party scripts, fonts or images.
  • Our host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and time of request, for a short period to keep the site running.

Email

If you write to us, for example to report a security issue, we use your message and address only to reply. We don't add you to any list.

Changes and questions

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will say so at the top. Questions are welcome at privacy@figlock.com.

FigLock is in development and nothing is released yet. This page describes our plan and will be reviewed before the first release.
