# Code of conduct

We want FigLock to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Respect other people's time: keep issues and pull requests focused and well described.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions, and may ban contributors, for behaviour that
breaks these standards. To report a problem, email
[security@figlock.com](mailto:security@figlock.com) with "conduct" in the subject, or message a
maintainer privately on GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
